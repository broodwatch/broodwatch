Six connected threat domains

Track criminal innovation from the adversary's perspective.

The platform is designed to observe how threat actors adapt, where new methods intersect with the financial system, and which developments warrant earlier executive attention.

Threat Domain

AI-enabled fraud and deception

Monitor the rapid adoption of generative tools, synthetic identities, impersonation methods, and automation that increase scale, precision, and speed across fraud campaigns.

Threat Domain

Cryptocurrency and illicit value transfer

Identify the infrastructure, wallet behavior, off-ramp patterns, and institutional exposure associated with scams, money laundering, and fast-moving cross-border movement of value.

Threat Domain

Threat Domain

Analytical workflow

From signals to insight.

BROODWATCH combines collection, structured analysis, adversarial review, and executive reporting into one cycle designed to produce earlier warning and clearer decisions.

Collect

Gather open-source, overseas, domestic, and specialist reporting relevant to emerging criminal techniques, digital infrastructure, and illicit-finance behavior.

Resolve and connect

Link names, domains, wallets, companies, platforms, places, and events to identify hidden relationships and broader patterns.

Challenge and assess

Pressure-test findings through red-team review, compare competing explanations, and assess novelty, foreign nexus, exposure, and velocity.

Brief decision-makers

Translate complexity into concise, readable intelligence output tailored to senior bank, fintech, compliance, or national-security leadership.

Operational value

Uncover new criminal methods before they become headlines.

BROODWATCH connects dark-web reporting, sanctions and hawala insights, cryptocurrency intelligence, AI-enabled fraud indicators, and transnational threat signals into an executive view designed for earlier recognition of material risk.

Independent development

Built outside formal duty to explore what next-generation threat intelligence can become.

BROODWATCH is an independent project developed by Todd Porinsky. It combines federal investigative experience, financial-crime expertise, and practical experimentation with AI-enabled analytical workflows to explore how earlier-warning intelligence can better serve financial institutions and national-security professionals.

PurposeAnticipate emerging criminal methods and technologies before they are widely recognized.
OrientationAdversary-centric, financially relevant, source-aware, and designed for executive decision-making.
Public siteThis website presents the concept only and does not expose investigative data, backend systems, or sensitive source material.
Designed for

Senior leaders who need clarity on emerging risk.

The BROODWATCH concept is relevant to banks, fintech firms, compliance teams, corporate investigations, strategic-intelligence functions, and national-security stakeholders who want earlier visibility into novel threats.
