    Analytical workflow

    From signals to insight.

    BROODWATCH combines collection, structured analysis, adversarial review, and executive reporting into one cycle designed to produce earlier warning and clearer decisions.

    Collect

    Gather open-source, overseas, domestic, and specialist reporting relevant to emerging criminal techniques, digital infrastructure, and illicit-finance behavior.

    Resolve and connect

    Link names, domains, wallets, companies, platforms, places, and events to identify hidden relationships and broader patterns.

    Challenge and assess

    Pressure-test findings through red-team review, compare competing explanations, and assess novelty, foreign nexus, exposure, and velocity.

    Brief decision-makers

    Translate complexity into concise, readable intelligence output tailored to senior bank, fintech, compliance, or national-security leadership.
