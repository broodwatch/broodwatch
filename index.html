The intelligence premise

Look beyond known typologies.

Traditional controls often focus on threats that have already been identified. BROODWATCH explores how emerging tactics, technologies, and criminal adaptations might be recognized earlier—before they become established patterns.

01 / DETECT

Find early signals

Organize diverse indicators of emerging criminal methods, infrastructure, and activity.

02 / CHALLENGE

Test assumptions

Use adversarial review to examine alternative explanations and reduce premature conclusions.

03 / BRIEF

Inform decisions

Translate complex findings into clear, evidence-aware assessments for executive audiences.

Analytical coverage

Six connected threat domains.

BROODWATCH is organized around intersecting financial-crime and national-security challenges.

DOMAIN 01

AI-Enabled Fraud

Emerging deception, automation, synthetic identities, and criminal uses of AI.

DOMAIN 02

Digital Assets

Cryptocurrency-enabled fraud, illicit settlement patterns, and wallet-linked activity.

DOMAIN 03

Sanctions Evasion

Hawala networks, shadow finance, intermediary structures, and cross-border methods.

DOMAIN 04

Ransomware

Criminal service ecosystems, monetization techniques, and operational shifts.

DOMAIN 05

Transnational Networks

Illicit supply chains, including synthetic-opioid precursor and enabling networks.

DOMAIN 06

Emerging Criminal Forums

Open-source indicators of evolving techniques and adversary discussions.

Independent development

Built from investigative experience. Designed for emerging threats.

BROODWATCH is an independent, self-directed project conceived and developed by Todd Porinsky, drawing on more than two decades of federal investigative experience involving transnational organized crime, financial crime, cryptocurrency, sanctions evasion, and ransomware.

The initiative explores how modern AI workflows can extend experienced investigative reasoning into earlier detection and clearer strategic communication.

BROODWATCH is an independent project and is not presented as an official U.S. government program or endorsement.
